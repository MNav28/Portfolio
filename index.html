Who i am

About me

I'm Michael, a Frontend Developer based in Munich, passionate about turning ideas into modern, intuitive, and functional web applications. What I particularly enjoy is the combination of creative design and technical implementation bringing individual components together to create a cohesive digital experience.

I'm based in Munich and flexible when it comes to working arrangements. Whether in the office, hybrid, or remotely, I'm open to different ways of working and look forward to collaborating with a dedicated team.

Web development is constantly evolving, and that's one of the things I find most exciting about it. I'm curious about new technologies and concepts and continuously work on expanding my skills to build modern and maintainable solutions.

When facing a challenge, I take a structured and solution-oriented approach. I analyze the problem, break it down into smaller steps, and explore different approaches until I find a clean and efficient solution. Analytical thinking, creativity, and persistence help me along the way.

Skill Set

I have a solid knowledge of modern frontend technologies and use them to build responsive, well-structured, and user-friendly web applications. I'm open to new frameworks, tools, and technologies and continuously strive to expand and deepen my skills.
